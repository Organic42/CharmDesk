CharmDesk

Last updated August 15, 2026

TL;DR — CharmDesk collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Summary

CharmDesk is a Windows desktop app that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no account system, no analytics, no ads, no telemetry, and does not connect to the internet at all. Everything the app stores, it stores only on your machine, and nothing ever leaves it.

What CharmDesk stores locally

To remember your preferences between launches, CharmDesk keeps a small set of files in your own user profile, under %AppData%\CharmDesk:

  • Settings — your selected charm, physics tweaks, sound toggle, and "start with Windows" preference, saved as a local JSON file.
  • Charm packages — the built-in charms and any you import yourself, stored as folders of image and manifest files.
  • Logs — a plain text log used only to help diagnose a crash, kept on your machine.

None of this is personal data in any meaningful sense — there's no name, email, account, or identifier attached to any of it — and none of it is ever transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app or deleting that folder removes all of it.

What CharmDesk does not do

  • No internet connection of any kind — CharmDesk makes zero network requests.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or user identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting sent off your device.
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to anyone, because none is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

CharmDesk does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If CharmDesk ever changes what it stores or how, this page will be updated and the date above will change accordingly.
