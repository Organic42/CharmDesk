CharmDesk

Last updated October 4, 2026

TL;DR — CharmDesk doesn't collect or send any personal data. Everything it saves stays on your PC.

Summary

CharmDesk is a Windows desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer never receives any information about you or how you use it.

The version you download from the CharmDesk website or GitHub never connects to the internet. The Microsoft Store version only contacts the Microsoft Store, to show tip prices and to process a tip if you choose to leave one. Both are explained below.

What CharmDesk saves on your PC

Everything is kept in your own user profile, in %AppData%\CharmDesk, and is never uploaded:

  • Settings (settings.json): which charm is shown, its size, position, screen and swing settings, your clock format, whether the charm is hidden or stays on top, whether CharmDesk starts with Windows, which built-in charms you've removed, and whether you've seen the welcome tips.
  • Charms (charms folder): the built-in charms and any you import, stored as image and settings files.
  • Log file (logs\charmdesk.log): a plain-text record used to diagnose problems. It notes how the app is drawing on your screen, any errors, and the times you schedule a shut down or sleep and whether it happened. Error messages can include file locations on your PC, and those locations often contain your Windows user name.

None of this includes your name, email, or any account or device identifier. Deleting the %AppData%\CharmDesk folder removes all of it.

What CharmDesk does on your PC

  • Start with Windows (off unless you turn it on): adds CharmDesk to your Windows startup apps. Turning it off removes it.
  • Shut down or sleep later: when you schedule it, CharmDesk asks Windows to shut down or sleep at the time you chose, after a one-minute warning you can cancel. The schedule is only kept while CharmDesk is running.
  • Copy diagnostic info (in Settings): only when you click it, this copies the app version, your Windows and .NET versions, a graphics capability level and the last 60 lines of the log file to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere; it's up to you where, or whether, you paste it.

Tips and the Microsoft Store

Tipping is optional, and the app works fully without it.

Version downloaded from the website or GitHub

The tip button opens a donation page in your web browser. What happens on that page is covered by that site's own privacy policy.

Microsoft Store version

  • When you open Settings, CharmDesk asks the Microsoft Store for the prices of the optional tips so it can show them in your currency.
  • If you leave a tip, the purchase is handled entirely by Microsoft. CharmDesk never sees your payment details.
  • Downloads, updates and purchases through the Store are covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft gives the developer summary reports, such as download and sales totals, that don't identify you.

Crash reports

CharmDesk doesn't send crash reports itself. As with any Windows app, if it crashes, Windows may send an error report to Microsoft, depending on your Windows diagnostic data settings. For the Microsoft Store version, Microsoft shares summary crash statistics with the developer to help fix problems.

What CharmDesk does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or user identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No personal data is collected, sold or shared, because CharmDesk doesn't collect any.

The CharmDesk website

The CharmDesk website and this page are hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children's privacy

CharmDesk doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If CharmDesk changes what it stores or how, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
