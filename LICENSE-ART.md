## What counts as artwork

The image files in these locations, along with the charm designs and characters they depict:

| Location | What it is |
|---|---|
| `charms/` | The charm art and thumbnails (`charm.png`, `thumbnail.png` in each charm folder) |
| `art-source/` | Source and work-in-progress art for the charms |
| `src/CharmDesk/Resources/` | The app icon |
| `packaging/Assets/` | Store tiles, logos and splash screen |
| `packaging/screenshots/` | Store screenshots |
| `docs/banner.png` | The project banner |

The charm `manifest.json` files are configuration, not artwork, and are covered by the MIT
License like the rest of the code.

## What you can do

- **Use CharmDesk**, including all the charms that come with it, on your own computers.
- **Share screenshots, videos and streams** that show CharmDesk running, including the charms.
  Reviews, social posts, tutorials and the like are all welcome.
- **Fork, clone and build this repository** for personal use, learning, or to contribute changes
  back to it. Your build may include the artwork while you are doing that.

## What you can't do without written permission

- Redistribute the artwork, whether on its own, in your own app, in a modified build of
  CharmDesk, or in charm packs.
- Sell the artwork, or anything that includes it.
- Use the artwork, or the charm characters it depicts, as the basis for your own designs or as
  branding for your own product.

If you want to do something that isn't covered here, ask: open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Organic42/CharmDesk/issues).
