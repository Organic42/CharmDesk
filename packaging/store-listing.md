## Description (main listing field, 10,000 char limit)

Little pixel-art charms that hang off the edge of your desktop — and swing like the real thing.

Phone charms and bag charms are everywhere right now. Your desktop, the one screen you stare at
all day, doesn't get to have any. CharmDesk fixes that: pin a tiny charm to the top of your
screen and it hangs there quietly, ready to be grabbed.

Every charm runs on a real 2D pendulum-spring physics simulation — not a looping animation.
Grab it with your mouse, pull it, let go, and it swings and settles like an actual object on a
string, with momentum that carries over naturally from the drag. Click it for a quick bounce,
double-click for a spin, or just leave it be and it'll sway on its own every so often.

WHAT'S IN THE BOX
• A small starting collection of charms — an evil eye, a gilded evil eye, a friendly ghost
• A Charm Library to browse, preview, and switch between them
• A Charm Manager to import your own PNG art and tune its physics, personality, and size on
  live sliders — no code, no rebuild
• Import and export charms as shareable .zip packs
• Four charm "personalities" (Default, Playful, Gentle, Dramatic) that change how a charm
  reacts to clicks and drags
• Lives quietly in the system tray — show/hide, switch charms, or reset position anytime

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY
CharmDesk's overlay is genuinely click-through everywhere except the charm itself, so it never
steals a click meant for your desktop or the app underneath it. It's a small, lightweight
background utility, not a resource hog.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
CharmDesk makes zero network requests. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, nothing sent
anywhere — settings and charm packs are stored locally and never leave your machine. Full
privacy policy: https://organic42.github.io/CharmDesk/privacy-policy.html
